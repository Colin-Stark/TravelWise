# TravelWise App - Product Backlog Creation Prompts 

## Overall Instruction for the AI

**Original Prompt**: "You are a highly experienced Agile Product Owner and Scrum Master. Your task is to guide the TravelWise team in creating a comprehensive product backlog document. You have access to the full business case proposal provided, which includes details on 'Key Features,' 'Preliminary Project Requirements,' 'Project Schedule,' 'Potential Risks,' 'Contingencies for Potential Risk,' and 'Technology Stack.' Your output should be structured to facilitate a hybrid agile approach, emphasizing user-friendliness and security throughout."

## Prompt Sequence for Product Backlog Creation

### Prompt 1 (Project Overview, Key Requirements & Epics Definition)

"Based on the provided TravelWise business case proposal, first, generate the 'Project Overview' including the project name and main objective. Next, list the 'Key Features' (from Section 5) and 'Preliminary Technical Requirements' (from Section 6) that will form the foundation. Finally, define and list all primary 'Epics' for the TravelWise application, grouping them logically by theme. Ensure the summary highlights the core value proposition and the importance of user experience and security."

### Prompt 2 (Sprint 1 & 2 Detailed Planning)

"Given the 'Development Phase' is 4 months (approximately 16 weeks) as per the 'Project Schedule' (Section 8), outline a 'Sprint Plan' structure for 4 sprints, each ~3 weeks. Then, for 'Sprint 1: Authentication, Profile & Dashboard Setup' and 'Sprint 2: Hotel, Flight & Transport Management', define the 'Sprint Goal', list 'Epics Covered', and write detailed 'User Stories'. For each user story, include specific 'Acceptance Criteria' that explicitly address both functional requirements and considerations for **security** and **user-friendliness/UX**."

### Prompt 3 (Sprint 3 & 4 Detailed Planning)

"Continuing the sprint planning, for 'Sprint 3: Booking Summary & Itinerary Management' and 'Sprint 4: AI Suggestions, Functional Testing, Accessibility & Polish', define the 'Sprint Goal', list 'Epics Covered', and write detailed 'User Stories'. For each user story, include specific 'Acceptance Criteria' that explicitly address both functional requirements and considerations for **security** and **user-friendliness/UX**."

### Prompt 4 (Risk Management & MVP Definition)

"Based on the 'Potential Risks' (Section 7) and 'Contingencies for Potential Risk' (Section 15) from the business case, describe how 'Risk Management' will be integrated 'by Sprint', providing examples of specific risks relevant to each sprint and outlining corresponding mitigation strategies. Finally, provide a clear 'MVP Definition' for the TravelWise application, listing the essential features and functionalities."

### Final Instruction for the AI (After all prompts have been run)

"Compile all the outputs from the previous prompts into a single, well-structured document titled 'TravelWise - Product Backlog Document'. Ensure it includes an introduction outlining the project overview, the hybrid agile approach, and emphasizes the integrated focus on security and user-friendliness. The document should be clear, concise, and ready for team review and execution."
